Our story

A high-school dream, 13 years in the making

Two school friends from Nashik always talked about opening a café of their own. Engineering, careers and other cities came first. When both moved back home, the dream finally got a front door at Canada Corner.

We named it after a bit of maths. Tan 90° tends to infinity, and that is what we want here: friendships that don't run out and flavours from across India that keep you curious. We serve regional favourites from Pune, Surat and Mumbai, all vegetarian, most of them available in a Jain version.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Café Tan 90° pure vegetarian?

Yes, 100%. Many items come in a Jain version, and we can make Swaminarayan-style food (no onion, no garlic) on request.

Where exactly are you in Nashik?

Shop No. 11, Ameya Sankul, Ramdas Colony, Sharanpur Road, Canada Corner. We're within walking distance of Allen, Physicswallah Vidyapeeth and Arihant Academy.

Can I host a birthday here?

Yes. We host birthdays, anniversaries and small get-togethers on the first floor, with decoration. Call or WhatsApp +91 83909 76818 to book.

What is Bhartiya Baithak?

It's our Indian floor-seating area, with low tables and cushions, made for long chats, group study and board games.

Why the name tan 90°?

In maths, tan 90° tends to infinity. For us it means infinite bonds and infinite tastes.

Are you offering franchises?

Yes, enquiries are open. See the franchise details or WhatsApp the founders.
